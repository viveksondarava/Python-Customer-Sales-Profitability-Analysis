\# Business Insights — Customer Sales \& Profitability Analysis



\## Executive Summary



The Python analysis examined 1,000 customer sales transactions across 2025, covering revenue, profit, product categories, regions, customer segments and monthly performance.



The analysis generated total revenue of £208,760.83 and total profit of £81,769.29, resulting in an overall profit margin of 39.17%.



\## Key Findings



\### 1. Overall Business Performance



\- Total Revenue: £208,760.83

\- Total Profit: £81,769.29

\- Profit Margin: 39.17%

\- Total Orders: 1,000

\- Total Units Sold: 2,933

\- Average Order Value: £208.76



The results indicate that the dataset represents a profitable retail operation, with profit generated across all major categories and regions.



\### 2. Category Performance



Electronics generated the highest revenue among the analysed categories at approximately £79.9K and also generated the highest profit at approximately £31.4K.



The category results were:



| Category | Revenue | Profit |

|---|---:|---:|

| Electronics | £79,881.50 | £31,426.00 |

| Office | £40,097.05 | £15,492.04 |

| Home | £37,166.85 | £14,691.85 |

| Fitness | £28,223.84 | £11,119.87 |

| Accessories | £23,391.59 | £9,039.53 |



Electronics therefore represents the largest contributor to both revenue and profit in the dataset.



\### 3. Regional Performance



London generated the highest revenue at approximately £40.7K and the highest profit at approximately £15.6K.



Regional performance:



| Region | Revenue | Profit |

|---|---:|---:|

| London | £40,666.31 | £15,632.91 |

| Leeds | £35,862.43 | £13,748.27 |

| Manchester | £35,227.64 | £13,581.31 |

| Bristol | £33,276.33 | £13,313.13 |

| Sheffield | £32,960.92 | £13,105.31 |

| Birmingham | £30,767.20 | £12,388.36 |



The relatively close profit levels across regions indicate that profitability is distributed across the business rather than concentrated in a single location.



\### 4. Customer Segment Performance



Consumer customers generated the highest revenue and profit among the three customer segments.



| Customer Segment | Revenue | Profit | Orders |

|---|---:|---:|---:|

| Consumer | £106,934.00 | £42,168.93 | 501 |

| Corporate | £63,699.09 | £24,727.28 | 306 |

| Small Business | £38,126.94 | £14,873.08 | 193 |



The Consumer segment accounted for approximately half of total revenue and orders, making it an important segment for continued analysis.



\### 5. Monthly Performance



Monthly revenue varied throughout 2025.



The highest monthly revenue occurred in August at approximately £22.7K.



The lowest monthly revenue occurred in December at approximately £12.9K.



This variation suggests that monthly sales patterns should be monitored to understand seasonal demand and identify opportunities for improving weaker periods.



\### 6. Top Products



The highest-revenue products included:



1\. Monitor — £22,202.68

2\. Keyboard — £19,813.21

3\. Webcam — £19,777.21

4\. Wireless Headphones — £18,088.40

5\. Notebook — £11,418.10



These products represent important contributors to overall sales performance and could be examined further for pricing, demand and inventory decisions.



\## Business Recommendations



Based on the analysis:



\- Continue monitoring Electronics performance because it is the largest revenue and profit contributor.

\- Investigate the factors behind London's strong regional performance.

\- Analyse Consumer purchasing behaviour to understand the largest customer segment.

\- Review monthly sales patterns to identify potential seasonal opportunities.

\- Monitor high-revenue products to support inventory and product planning.

\- Compare revenue and profit together when evaluating product and regional performance.



\## Conclusion



The project demonstrates how Python can be used to transform raw transactional data into structured business analysis.



The analysis combines data-quality checks, KPI calculations, category analysis, regional analysis, customer-segment analysis, monthly trends and product analysis to support data-driven business decision-making.
